# Impulsa Real Estate
User brand takes precedence over general UI UX Pro Max recommendations.
Colors: gold #AF9768, dark gold #8C754E, charcoal #302F28, black #181918, gray #E8E8E8, white #FFFFFF.
Typography: Montserrat for interface, Cormorant Garamond for editorial titles.
Direction: architectural editorial. Large photographs, restrained fine borders, generous white space. No gratuitous gradients. Black text on gold buttons for contrast. Fine gold eyebrow labels, serif headings, dark immersive hero.
Home: narrow service strip, compact navigation, large hero, overlapping search, curated listings, destinations, services, people, conversion.
Accessibility: semantic forms, visible focus, labels, reduced motion, keyboard dialogs, 44px minimum targets.
Images are illustrative demo photography, not verified Dominican listings. Demo data must be identified.
